  SEG lateral tibial rim (Segond) on coronal; FIB fibular head and styloid; SPN tibial spines / intercondylar eminence; TPL medial and lateral tibial plateau, subchondral and posterior rim, on sagittal and coronal; FEM femoral condyles, subchondral bone and the lateral condylar notch, on sagittal and coronal; PAT patella (medial facet, inferior pole, body) on axial and sagittal; TUB tibial tubercle and proximal tibia.

- pf_oa: native axial through the patellar facets and the trochlea and native sagittal through the patellofemoral joint, cartilage enlarged. Estimate the deepest loss as a percent of the local full cartilage thickness, and its extent in mm with the 10 mm bar. Start the pf_oa note with "d~N% e~M mm;" (N = deepest loss in percent, M = extent of the loss deeper than half the thickness, 0 if none), then your usual note.
- The note limit is 200 characters: keep the rest of each note short.
