No web search. Do not try to find out which real study an index belongs to. Judge every study fresh from its images.

Native full-resolution views (view.py), a fixed protocol per study:
- first run view.py series INDEX to see the series; ignore series marked "other knee"; for cartilage prefer proton-density / intermediate-weighted series (fat-suppressed or not) and the thinnest slices;
- coronal: every slice of the best coronal series from the front to the back of the lateral femoral condyle's weight-bearing surface, cropped to the lateral half around the joint line (--box 0.45 0.25 1 0.75; coronal tiles have lateral on the right); at most 12 slices per show call;
- sagittal: every slice of the best sagittal series through the lateral femoral condyle, from the first slice lateral to the intercondylar notch (where the notch and cruciates disappear) to the lateral edge of the condyle (the stack runs medial to lateral), cropped to the femorotibial joint (--box 0.15 0.2 0.85 0.8); at most 12 slices per show call;
- use the default window; when bright fluid hides the cartilage edge, repeat the slices that matter with --window 5 99.9.
Judge depth against the neighbouring normal cartilage of the same surface, where the surface is cut at a right angle. Measure extent_mm with the 10 mm bar of the native view, or as the number of consecutive slices with deep loss times the slice spacing that view.py series prints. Write view PNGs only in your temp folder.

Looking: view every panel sheet and every native view you rely on with view_image at full resolution. Code may only display, crop, enlarge or change brightness and contrast. Never decide or pre-fill labels with code. Evidence and boxes must name bundle tiles; when something is clearest on a native view, box it on the bundle tile of the same plane with the nearest position and name the view in the note, e.g. "native s4 #18-21".
