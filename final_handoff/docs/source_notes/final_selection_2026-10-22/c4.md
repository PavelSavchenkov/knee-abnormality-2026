## 2. C4 reader record on LB behaviour (session b8f89321, 10-19 20:19)

Source: work/brainstorm_2026-10-18/overhaul/c4_refit_q25.md. It covers 20 anchored single-column LB reads up to 10-19 20:14.
It does NOT include the 10-20 / 10-21 / 10-22 probes; no one re-fit it on those.

- **Readers do not rank probes.**
  - Leave-one-read-out sign accuracy: old C4 12 / 20, new fits 10-11, G only 9 / 16, T only 8 / 18, H only 6 / 11.
  - 12 / 20 is just the share of winning probes. Every probe was submitted because its readers were positive, so C4 acts as
    "always +".
- **Single LB reads are noisy (sd ~35-55e-5).** Even a perfect local reader would match LB signs on only ~11-15 / 20.
  - My arithmetic, not in the record: the difference of two such reads has sd ~50-80e-5. One LB gap below ~0.001 between two
    packages is weak evidence.
- **Only gold SIZE separated winners from losers, and only a little:** AUC 0.71, Spearman +0.41, n 15.
  - Mean G was +10.0 on wins vs +6.2 on losses.
  - T (AUC 0.42) and H (0.48) did not separate them.
- **Fitted-to rule.** If an arm moves TOWARD a reader (adds that reader's labels / pairs as targets, or up-weights a part trained
  on them), that reader cannot rank it.
  - For "image-read-trained heads vs text-label heads": Codex / Opus image readers cannot judge the image-read heads, and text
    readers cannot judge the text heads. Lead with gold (n+, pairs, movers) there, plus the per-column LB record.
  - Like-for-like swaps (same target, other backbone or seed) keep that reader usable: both sides are equally fitted.
- **Weak LB record (n = 3) on the fitted-to rule:**
  - like-for-like Synovitis cnx crops, Codex-led: LB +18.7 (right);
  - moved toward Codex: Effusion img_eff w 0.4 (I +3.7, LB -14) and Q25 pw on Codex pairs (I +19.7, LB -100), both wrong.
- **Gold-led single-reader passes on F / C / PF matched the LB sign 1 / 3 times** (Contusion FS yes; Q20 and m_fs no). Treat this
  as a caveat, not a gate.
- **Retracted 10-19 ~22:05 (user):** the "image-reader tilt = LB loss" rule and the ban on image-teacher tie-break variants
  (the record's RK3 addendum). RK3 raised gold on its target columns, so it was a probe candidate, not a fail.
- **The family weights fitted in the record cannot be identified; do not use them.** The OA + menisci fit rests on H from only
  2 reads.

## 3. Caveats for using this in the final pick

- The record is about PUBLIC LB signs. The private test is a different split, so per-class public LB gains carry the same
  sampling noise again.
- n is small everywhere: 20 reads, 15 with gold, 3 fitted-to. None of these numbers is a pick rule. They describe how far each
  signal can be trusted.
